OpenPharmaStock is a privacy-conscious Android inventory utility for pharmacy
stock-counting workflows.

Features:

* Scan GS1 DataMatrix, EAN and other common barcodes
* Extract GTIN, serial, lot and expiry information when available
* Aggregate stock quantities and avoid duplicate serials
* Keep local inventory sessions and scan history
* Import a local medicine catalogue from CSV
* Export summaries as CSV or text
* Create and restore local backups

Inventory data stays on the device. Online INFARMED medicine lookup is disabled
by default and can be enabled explicitly in Settings. The scanner uses only
FLOSS components and does not upload camera frames.

OpenPharmaStock is an early release and is not a replacement for validated
professional pharmacy software.
